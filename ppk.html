Rules & Regulations

Welcome to PPAPP! To ensure a comfortable and conducive environment for all our visitors, we kindly request that you observe our library rules and regulations.

  • Please keep your voice down to maintain a quiet reading environment.
  • Food and drinks are strictly prohibited inside the reading areas.
  • Please handle all library materials, books, and equipment with care.
  • Ensure your personal belongings are kept secure at all times.

Thank you for your cooperation in making the library a pleasant place for everyone!
